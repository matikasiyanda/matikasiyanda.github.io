I train small language models to do things they weren't built for. Lately
that means a document-search agent: Qwen3-1.7B + LoRA, trained with GRPO on a
single RTX 4090, that learned to reformulate queries, chain hops across
documents and say "not here" when the answer isn't in the corpus.
[The write-up is here](/blog/agent-rl/).
